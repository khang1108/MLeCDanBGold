\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An audit of 40 queries (113 events) separates video-selection and event-alignment failures in an existing multimodal pipeline.
Only a quarter of queries are fully correct even with the correct video, and within-video frame ranking remains a bottleneck in the variants studied.
Video-score margins discriminate first-pass video errors, but an unflagged result is not safe to accept without inspection.
Under correct-video oracle feedback, five event-level actions raise correctness from 25\% to 70\%, mostly through the DP starting path and order constraints; counting wrong videos as failures gives 10\% to 35\%.
These results quantify correction headroom, while human effort and combined routing effectiveness remain open.
The frozen query manifest, annotations, cached scores, and evaluation scripts will be released upon publication.
