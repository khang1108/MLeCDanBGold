\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

An event-by-event audit shows that, even with the correct video, only a quarter of queries are fully correct; within the variants studied, errors stem mainly from how frames inside a video are ranked.
The margin between the top two videos separates correct from wrong first passes (AUROC 0.85), and, with the correct video and simulated oracle feedback, five event-level actions raise whole-sequence correctness from 25\% to 70\%, mostly owing to the DP starting path and order constraints.
Annotations, cached score matrices, and scripts will be released upon publication.
