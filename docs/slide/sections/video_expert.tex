\section{VideoExpert: Augmented LLM for Temporal-Sensitive Video Understanding}

\subsection{Abstract}
\begin{frame}{Abstract}
    \begin{block}{Challenge}
        MLLMs tend to rely more on \textbf{language patterns} than visual cues when generating timestamps.
    \end{block}

    \begin{block}{Proposed Solution}
        \texttt{VideoExpert}, an MLLM for serveral \textbf{temporal-sensitive} video tasks. 

        It has twoo parallel modules:
        \begin{itemize}
            \item \textbf{Temporal Expert} models time sequences and performing temporal grounding. It compresses tokens to capture dynamic variations in videos and predict \textbf{event localization}.
            \textbf{Spatial Expert} focuses on content detail analysis. It handles designed spatial tokens and language input to generat content-related responses.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{block}
\end{frame}
