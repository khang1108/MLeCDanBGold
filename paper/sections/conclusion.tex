\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented SHI, an interactive multimodal video retrieval system for
multi-event KIS. The pre-retrieval Query Hypothesis makes the LLM-generated
event decomposition editable, while the post-retrieval EventTrail re-decodes the
temporal alignment of a candidate video under operator-supplied anchor and
exclusion constraints. We will evaluate both mechanisms under live task
conditions at VBS 2027.
