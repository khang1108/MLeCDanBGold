\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Temporal alignment for event retrieval.}
DANTE and TARS use monotone DP with running or prefix maxima~\cite{luu2025dante,do2025tars}; KPTER uses K-pointer reranking~\cite{dinh2025kpter}; and Cross Segment Coherence Scoring penalizes order reversals, gaps, and shot jumps~\cite{nguyen2025cscs}.
Other approaches combine DP and agents~\cite{nguyen2025agentdp}, use beam search~\cite{vu2025madtempo}, or add LVLM verification~\cite{ho2025lucifertrace}.
Training-free temporal grounding also decomposes queries and filters VLM proposals by event order~\cite{zheng2024tfvtg}.
Our evaluation separates video selection from event placement and measures correction under a limited budget, without claiming a universally better temporal model.

\paragraph{Query performance prediction.}
Text-retrieval predictors include query clarity~\cite{cronen2002predicting}, weighted information gain~\cite{zhou2007wig}, and normalized query commitment~\cite{shtok2012nqc}; see~\cite{carmel2010difficulty} for a survey.
In video search, Kofler et al.\ predict failure from logs and visual results~\cite{kofler2012failure}, while VQPP benchmarks text-to-video prediction and query reformulation~\cite{lutu2026vqpp}.
We evaluate established score signals on jointly decoded event paths without session history, distinguishing correct video selection from correct placement of all events.

\paragraph{Interaction and correction.}
Interactive video search systems refine their results through relevance feedback, conversation, and query history~\cite{khan2024exquisitor,sharma2025exquisitor,ma2022ivcmr}.
At SoICT 2025, interactive mechanisms refine the query: an LLM agent through dialogue~\cite{mai2025conversational}, composed-image textual feedback~\cite{to2025cirfeedback}, or Rocchio feedback~\cite{nguyen2025vortex}.
Our correction edits the decoded path.
Constrained decoding, propagation, and confidence-ordered simulated corrections predate this work in interactive information extraction~\cite{kristjansson2004constrained}.
Keep/Use/Reject actions are inherited from SHI's EventTrail~\cite{nguyen2027shi}; our contribution is annotation and controlled analysis of initialization, ordering, proposals, and propagation.
Section~\ref{sec:correction} specifies the protocol independently of that manuscript.
