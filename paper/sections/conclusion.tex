\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented SHI, an interactive multimodal video retrieval system for multi-event
KIS at VBS 2027. Rather than treating narrative decomposition and temporal alignment
as opaque black-box processes, SHI externalizes two intermediate interaction points:
the pre-retrieval Query Hypothesis for editable narrative structuring, and the
post-retrieval EventTrail for sub-second occurrence steering and path re-decoding via
Evidence Snapshots. Empirical results show that dual-stage hypothesis intervention
recovers from early cascade errors, boosting timestamp accuracy from 60\% to 90\%.
Future work will evaluate operator dynamics in live competition at VBS 2027.
